Lorenzo Vigotti is an architect and architectural historian who received an M.Arch. from the University of Florence and a Ph.D. in architectural history from Columbia University. During a two-year postdoctoral fellowship at the Kunsthistorisches Institut in Florence, Lorenzo developed [Pupilli](https://www.pupilli.org), an archival research project examining the material culture and domestic life of late medieval and early modern Florence through more than 3,000 previously unpublished household inventories. He directed the virtual reconstruction of Florence’s now-lost Jewish ghetto for an exhibition at the Uffizi Galleries and received a 3-year EU grant to study Medieval domes in Iran. Lorenzo has taught courses on Western and Islamic architecture and urbanism at Columbia University, New York University, Stanford University in Florence, Pratt Institute, the University of Utah, and the University of Bologna.
