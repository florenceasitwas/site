Kelan Joiner '27 is an engineering major from Social Circle, Georgia. He creates 3D photogrammetry and point cloud models for FLAW. He co-authored a _Heritage_ published article alongside Dave Pfaff and Dr. George Bent, titled, ["A Digital Reconstruction of the Tramezzo and Presbytery in S. Remigio, Florence."](https://wlu.box.com/s/lyk5o2cugga8tlca16ff1zd6ar80u9a9) On campus, he is a member of Pi Kappa Alpha Fraternity and a member of the Interfraternity Council executive board. Outside of class he enjoys reading, weightlifting, running, and being outdoors.
